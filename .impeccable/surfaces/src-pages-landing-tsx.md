# Surface brief — MANDATE landing + live console

Scope: `src/pages/Landing.tsx` (Persuade) hands off into `src/pages/Console.tsx` (Operate). One world, two modes.

- Audience: hackathon judges first; then CISO (buyer), SOC analyst (daily user), agent business owner.
- Action: run the live console and watch an attack get caught and contained.
- Proof: the in-browser engine's real scoring output, labelled synthetic telemetry; cited public figures only with attribution.
- Constraints: no fabricated customers, measured rates, or pricing commitments; colour never the sole risk signal; reduced motion respected.
- Memorable moment: the IAM row stamps ALLOWED on every step while the risk pen climbs to the threshold and the agent is scoped down, work still flowing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: MANDATE is a polygraph for AI agents: the declared role is the claim, and the chart shows exactly where behaviour stops telling the truth. It refuses the category default of dark glass panels, a glowing node graph and KPI tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Gridded chart paper (cool chart white, millimetre grid in recorder green, heavier every fifth line) mounted in a hammertone instrument housing of deep green-black enamel. One ink per lens: black Mandate, amber Taint, blue Self, violet Herd, green Chain; red ink reserved for risk and alarm. Engraved condensed caps on the housing; chart numerals and event marks in a technical mono. Controls are engraved push-buttons, detent selectors, toggles and paper-advance keys.

STORY: The visitor watches a valid-credential agent drift while every access check passes, understands the three circles (granted, declared, observed), believes the score because each factor is annotated on the chart, sees containment keep work flowing, then opens the console and runs the attacks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed housing. Top: engraved nameplate strip with model line and chart speed. Left ~40%: headline "The token is valid. The behaviour isn't." in large condensed display, one-line deck, engraved primary push-button "Run the live console", secondary "Read a Mandate Card". Right ~60%, bleeding off the right edge: the live paper strip scrolling right to left, six labelled channels plus the red RISK pen, an IAM row stamping ALLOWED on every step, event marks T0–T4, and red stamps SCOPED DOWN then DECOY SERVED at threshold. Signature interaction: a reading crosshair that scrubs the chart and reads every channel and factor at that instant. Motion grammar: constant-speed paper advance, pens easing to value, stamps landing in 120 ms; nothing else moves.

FORM: The Chart Recorder (analog polygraph / multi-pen strip-chart recorder), position 3 of the ordered grounded list, seed key b179a649.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Raises carried from declined challengers:
- From HyperCard: the Mandate Card reads and amends in place; an owner amendment writes its own receipt to the Change Ledger.
- From Teletext: every agent and incident carries a short keyed address you can jump to from the keyboard.
- From Cassette Deck: every response is its own engraved detent on one selector; no containment action hides in a menu.
- From Futurist Book: annotation type scales with each factor's contribution to the score.
- From Glaze River: the drift budget fills toward a marked shelf line, so distance to threshold is always visible.
- From Provenance Ribbon: observed facts are drawn solid; inferred attributions are hairline.
